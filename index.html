About

Creating images that move people.

Aldrin Sharon Antony is a cinematographer, photographer and video editor based in Kerala, specialising in visual storytelling for advertisements, promotional campaigns and social media content.

His approach combines camera movement, composition, lighting and post-production with an understanding of the story behind each brand. From commercial product films to sports and lifestyle content, every frame is built to communicate with purpose.
